# Unassignment clears ownership, not future eligibility

The owner corrected the earlier blanket ban on reclaim by a removed agent.
Any otherwise eligible agent, including the former owner, may explicitly claim or
adopt unowned preserved work. An explicit exclusion still applies. Agents cannot
independently steal another active claim; trusted human direction may authorize
reassignment while preserving work and provenance.

Amended the release contract and native acceptance matrix. #1018 owns the policy
change and #1019 its integration. This documentation does not silently change
runtime behavior, resume another agent's claim, or implement the generic force flag.
